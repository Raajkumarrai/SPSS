Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities

Our Commitment to Privacy

At SPPS (Student Performance Prediction System), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and login credentials
  • Academic information (student ID, department, year)
  • Performance data (attendance, assignments, grades)

Usage Data

  • System interactions and feature usage
  • Prediction requests and results
  • Technical logs and error reports
  • Device and browser information

How We Use Your Information

Primary Purposes

  • Generate accurate performance predictions
  • Provide personalized educational insights
  • Improve our machine learning algorithms
  • Enhance system functionality and user experience

Analytics and Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to optimize performance
  • Develop new features and capabilities
  • Conduct research to improve educational outcomes
  • Ensure system security and reliability

Data Security

Security Measures

  • End-to-end encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure database storage with access controls
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Multi-factor authentication for system access

Data Protection

  • Anonymization of data for research purposes
  • Regular data backups and disaster recovery
  • Staff training on data protection protocols
  • Incident response procedures for security breaches

Information Sharing

We Do Not Share

  • Personal information with third parties
  • Student data with external organizations
  • Individual performance data publicly
  • Contact information for marketing purposes

Limited Sharing

  • Aggregated, anonymized data for research
  • System administrators for technical support
  • Legal compliance when required by law
  • Emergency situations for safety purposes

Your Rights

Data Access and Control

  • View and download your personal data
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-out of data processing activities

Privacy Controls

  • Manage your privacy settings
  • Control data sharing preferences
  • Request data portability
  • File complaints about data handling

Data Retention

Retention Periods

  • Active user data: Until account deletion
  • Inactive accounts: 2 years after last login
  • System logs: 1 year for security purposes
  • Research data: 5 years in anonymized form

Data Deletion

  • Automatic deletion after retention periods
  • Secure data destruction procedures
  • Verification of complete data removal
  • Notification of data deletion completion
